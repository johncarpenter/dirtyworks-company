# Target-account research schema

**Purpose:** build a focused, evidence-based list for founder-led outreach.  
**Initial list size:** 100 accounts in one sponsor-selected cluster.

## Required fields

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Account name and URL | Legal/trading name and primary site |
| Location(s) | Alberta office/service footprint |
| Vertical and subvertical | Use a consistent controlled label |
| Employee-size signal | Range and source/date; do not invent precision |
| Revenue/scale proxy | Optional; projects, locations, fleet, job volume, or public estimate with source |
| Operating complexity | Practices, teams, offices, projects, client groups, field groups, or role boundaries |
| Likely knowledge domains | Firm methods, engagement/project administration, approved templates, client requirements, systems procedures, project closeout, commercial knowledge |
| Technology signals | Microsoft/Google, practice/project/field-service/ERP/CRM systems, job postings, partner badges |
| Trigger event | Hiring, acquisition, expansion, leadership change, new system, retirement, compliance/quality event |
| Trigger source/date | Public link or founder-supplied evidence |
| Likely economic buyer | Name, title, confidence, source |
| Likely champion/knowledge holder | Name/title or role hypothesis |
| Warm path | Mutual contact and relationship strength; keep private |
| Fit score | Use ICP evidence, not enthusiasm |
| Personalization hypothesis | One sentence tied to observed operating context |
| Contact state | Researching, intro requested, contacted, replied, interview, qualified, closed |
| Next action / owner / date | One explicit action |
| Exclusion reason | Record why an account is removed |

## Evidence rules

- Source volatile facts and record the observation date.
- Separate observed facts from hypotheses.
- Do not scrape or store sensitive personal information.
- Use business contact information and reasonable professional outreach.
- Prefer 30 well-researched relevant accounts over 1,000 unqualified names.
- Keep founder relationship notes access-controlled.
- Recheck trigger and role data before contact.

## Initial segmentation

After choosing the first direct subsegment, define:

- included industry codes/descriptions;
- included geography;
- employee/scale signals;
- excluded regulated or high-risk work;
- priority trigger events;
- three likely answer domains;
- known platform/source systems;
- credible warm networks and associations.

For accounting or HR/advisory cohorts, explicitly exclude target use cases that require client records, employee/candidate data, professional conclusions, or employment decisions. For energy-service cohorts, exclude safety-critical, engineering, control-system, field-action, and regulatory-decision use cases.

Do not begin account collection until these inclusion rules are recorded; otherwise the list will drift horizontally.
