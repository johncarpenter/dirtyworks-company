# Business operating scorecard

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Status:** Definitions established; no operating data yet

## Purpose

Manage Dirty Works as an evidence-producing service business. The scorecard separates activity, conversion, customer outcomes, delivery quality, economics, risk, and founder dependence. A strong number in one area must not conceal failure in another.

## Metric rules

- Record raw numerator and denominator, not only a percentage.
- Segment by cohort, offer, customer size, platform, source complexity, and risk when sample size permits.
- Do not turn an early target into an external promise.
- Founder time receives a fully loaded cost even before payroll is taken.
- Pass-through licences are excluded from service revenue and gross margin when Dirty Works does not earn or carry meaningful value/risk.
- Averages never override critical permission, privacy, security, or safety failures.
- Usage is a leading indicator, not proof of value.
- Customer-reported time saved is labeled as an estimate unless independently measured.

|---|---|

## Operating meetings

### Weekly founder review during phases 0–2

- interviews, opportunities, proposals, and loss mechanisms;
- active delivery gates and critical risks;
- cash commitments and capacity for the next four weeks;
- assumptions strengthened or disproven;
- one decision and three next actions.

### Monthly business review

- funnel and cohort conversion;
- revenue, margin, cash, concentration, and capacity;
- customer outcome, quality, incidents, and retention risk;
- change work and scope leakage;
- roadmap gate evidence;
- sponsor decisions or help required;
- documentation, tool, and application changes.

### Quarterly strategy review

- ICP and vertical evidence;
- offer and willingness-to-pay evidence;
- standalone service economics and expansion evidence;
- platform/vendor shifts;
- privacy, regulation, insurance, and risk posture;
- founder role and hiring trigger;
- continue, narrow, expand, redesign, or stop each major hypothesis.

## Data-system sequence

Start with a simple structured table while volume is low. Introduce CRM, service desk, finance integration, and dashboards when manual reconciliation becomes unreliable or consumes measured time. The definitions in this document should survive a tool change.
