# Company-building log

**Purpose:** preserve the reasoning trail for decisions, assumptions, sponsor questions, blockers, failed attempts, and work that cannot continue.  
**Started:** 2026-08-25

## Status definitions

- **Working decision:** reversible choice used to keep execution moving; sponsor may change it.
- **Approved decision:** sponsor-confirmed or contractually committed choice.
- **Superseded decision:** retained for history but replaced by a later decision.
- **Assumption:** unverified belief with a test and expiry/review point.
- **Sponsor question:** meaningful choice that benefits from sponsor judgment but is not yet blocking safe work.
- **Blocker:** execution cannot responsibly continue on the affected work.
- **Unable to proceed:** safe in-scope alternatives are exhausted; required help is explicit.

## Change log

### 2026-08-26 — Per-client Markdown operating source selected

- Replaced Hudu as the launch-default private operating record with one private Git-backed Markdown repository per end customer; retained Hudu as an evidence-triggered migration option.
- Inspected the local `personal-wiki` and `kgmd` projects. Confirmed that personal-wiki's configurable version-2 page types, fixed frontmatter, Git proposal review and source lineage can support the first schema without a frontmatter-model change.
- Defined authored account, requirement, service, product, access, process, training, cost, risk, decision, change, incident, review, person and organization lifecycles; synthesized finding/source lifecycles; and a controlled directed relationship grammar.
- Preserved personal-wiki's authored/synthesized boundary: ingestion proposes cited findings and a human explicitly promotes accepted findings into authored operational state, avoiding a hybrid page type or automated overwrite of current records.
- Identified that personal-wiki does not enforce required sections on authored pages; specified a deterministic operational lint and complete authored scaffolds rather than weakening the record contract or treating this as an undocumented guarantee.
- Identified that current kgmd includes all Markdown beneath an included directory, treats frontmatter as chunk text and relies on LLM extraction for relationships; specified a client wrapper that excludes reserved pages, parses metadata and controlled edges deterministically, and labels inferred edges.
- Validated the proposed 17-type recipe contract with the actual local personal-wiki models and CLI; an authored requirement scaffold initialized with every required heading, lint returned no findings, and direct authoring of a synthesized finding was refused as intended.
- Defined personal-wiki as the governed publication/source layer and kgmd as a rebuildable per-client graph/MCP read layer; derived graph output cannot authorize action or overwrite published facts.
- Created a template pack with schema, ingestion prompt, kgmd corpus configuration, ignore rules and thirteen authored record templates.
- Preserved Freshdesk, 1Password and native contract/vendor/identity/finance authority boundaries and the manual monthly cost-reconciliation decision.
- Exposed two production gates: durable Git retention/deletion for imported originals and approved model-provider/data-classification policy for personal-wiki/kgmd processing.
- No blocker was opened. These choices do not prevent template/tool development or a synthetic two-client test.
