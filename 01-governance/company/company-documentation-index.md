## Document authority

When documents conflict, use this order:

1. A dated decision in the company-building log.
2. The master business strategy.
3. The offer, GTM, delivery, and roadmap documents.
4. The business model canvas and research notes.

Pricing and service promises are hypotheses until the strategy records them as validated. Public material must never turn an unvalidated target, outcome, or service level into a guarantee.

## Working conventions

- Currency is CAD unless stated otherwise.
- Separate **evidence**, **inference**, **assumption**, and **decision**.
- Give every phase a measurable gate and a stop condition.
- Keep customer production accounts and data customer-owned by default.
- Record a blocker when work cannot continue, including the attempted path, impact, required help, owner, and next review date.
- Record sponsor decisions separately from blockers. A reversible working assumption should not stop execution.
- Do not build proprietary software merely because it is possible. Build when repeated delivery evidence shows a productized component will improve margin, reliability, or differentiation.
- Do not count logins, prompts, or model tokens as business outcomes by themselves.
