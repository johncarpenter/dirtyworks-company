# Initial segment prioritization

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Decision needed:** choose the first concentrated 10-interview direct cohort  
**Options:** accounting firms, HR/advisory firms, or Calgary-area energy-service/vendor SMBs

## Recommendation

Start with **Calgary-area energy-service/vendor SMBs** if the founder or live MSP can produce at least 10 credible conversations in that ecosystem. Use **accounting firms** as the first alternative when access is materially stronger. Defer **HR/advisory firms** to the second or later cohort unless a clear network advantage exists.

This is a recommendation, not an approved decision. Access can outweigh theoretical segment quality during an early founder-led experiment.

## Mechanism

The first cohort should maximize five things at once:

1. repeated, economically meaningful knowledge-access events;
2. useful existing source material;
3. ability to bound a low-risk first domain;
4. buyer willingness and ability to fund a recurring managed service;
5. enough founder/MSP access to complete comparable interviews.

## Option comparison

| Dimension | Accounting firms | HR/advisory firms | Energy-service/vendor SMBs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge density | High: methods, engagements, templates, software, tax/audit practice material | High: policies, jurisdictional material, templates, service methods | High: commercial, project, client, system, and lessons-learned material |
| Likely pain | Seasonal onboarding, partner/manager interruptions, standardization, changing procedures | Consultant consistency, onboarding, policy/template retrieval | Project handoffs, client requirements, proposals, closeout, key-person dependence |
| Budget potential | Moderate; efficiency-sensitive and concentrated market | Moderate; many firms may be small | Moderate-high in established service vendors |
| Safe first scope | Internal firm operations and engagement administration | Internal methods and administration | Commercial/project administration and internal systems |
| Main excluded risk | Client financial data, tax/audit conclusions, professional judgment | Employee/candidate data, legal interpretation, employment decisions | Safety, engineering, field actions, operational control, environmental/regulatory decisions |
| Procurement friction | Usually low–moderate | Usually low | Moderate; rises quickly with larger energy clients |
| Regional differentiation | Moderate | Low–moderate | High in Calgary/Alberta |
| MSP channel fit | Strong Microsoft/security/document footprint | Strong but privacy-sensitive | Strong tenant/security/integration need; partner credibility valuable |
| Dirtyworks.ai brand fit | Good if visual identity is professional | Requires strongest trust presentation | Natural “hard operational work” association |
| Initial recommendation | Second / fallback first | Third | First if access threshold is met |

## Supporting evidence

Small businesses represented 48.9% of Alberta employment in professional, scientific, and technical services in the province’s 2023 profile data. Alberta also represented 12.3% of Canadian accounting-services operating revenue in 2024. [Alberta Small Business Profile](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/cc1c3dcf-d39e-4afa-9eff-32bda3759b79/resource/b60aeadc-c7bd-41ef-9115-c6b3a1f8588b/download/jet-alberta-small-business-profile-2024-10.pdf) and [Statistics Canada accounting services](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/251110/dq251110b-eng.pdf)

Alberta businesses also demonstrate relatively high outsourcing behavior, and outsourcing increases in the 20–99 and 100+ employee bands. Mining, quarrying, and oil-and-gas extraction firms were relatively frequent buyers of outside professional services. [Statistics Canada outsourcing analysis](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2025006-eng.htm)

These sources establish market plausibility, not the knowledge problem or willingness to pay. Interviews and paid reviews remain the decision evidence.

## Selection rule

Choose a cohort only when all are true:

- at least 30 plausible target accounts can be identified;
- the founder/MSP can name a path to 10 conversations within six weeks;
- three low-risk question domains can be hypothesized without using excluded data/decisions;
- likely customer size supports the $2,500 review and $2,500+ monthly hypothesis;
- one owner/operations/partner buyer role can be targeted consistently.

If no segment passes, narrow by relationship network rather than broadening the category.

## Cohort boundaries

### Recommended energy-service cohort

- Calgary-area service, engineering-support, environmental, maintenance, equipment/service technology, project, or specialized vendor firms;
- 20–150 employees as a working band;
- exclude major producers and heavily enterprise-procured accounts from cohort 1;
- avoid first use in safety, engineering judgment, field control, or regulatory decisions.

### Accounting alternative

- independent regional accounting, bookkeeping/payroll, tax, or advisory firms;
- 20–150 employees or multiple offices/practices;
- first domain in firm operations, software procedures, onboarding, templates, or engagement administration;
- exclude client financial records and professional conclusions from the initial system.

### HR/advisory alternative

- established outsourced HR, people advisory, recruiting operations, or organizational consulting firms;
- enough employees and repeatable methods to support shared knowledge;
- first domain in internal methods/templates/administration;
- exclude candidate/employee records, profiling, selection, performance, compensation, termination, and legal conclusions.
