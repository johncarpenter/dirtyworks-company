# Pre-customer acceptance checklist

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Owner:** Account owner  
**Status:** Working operational checklist  
**Use:** decide whether Dirtyworks.ai should accept a specific customer and scope before committing price, production date or privileged access

This is an acceptance gate, not a sales-discovery script. A promising prospect can be moved to **paid discovery** when value is plausible but product, data, risk or effort is not yet clear. It should not be forced into a managed-service quote merely to keep momentum.

## 1. Account fit and authority

- [ ] Legal customer name, operating name, location and contracting authority are known.
- [ ] Account fits the current professional-services, energy-services or approved MSP-sourced lane.
- [ ] Company size, user count, locations and expected growth fit the service model.
- [ ] Executive sponsor, day-to-day owner, access/security owner, finance owner and authorized signer are named.
- [ ] For an MSP-sourced account, prime/subcontractor, brand, customer-contact, ticket, escalation, billing and liability seams are written.
- [ ] The customer has authority over the proposed users, tenant, data and configuration.

## 2. Problem, outcome and use

- [ ] Recent triggering problem and current workaround are described with concrete examples.
- [ ] Desired business outcome can be stated without relying only on licences, prompts, logins or tokens.
- [ ] Initial scope is bounded by product(s), user group, business process and location.
- [ ] Supported uses, unsupported uses and required human verification are written.
- [ ] Customer acknowledges responsibility for policy, business decisions, access approvals and employee use.
- [ ] Success and 30-day review measures can be observed without disproportionate employee surveillance.

## 3. Product and technical fit

- [ ] Each requested product is on the supported list or has an owner for product approval.
- [ ] Customer-owned tenant and customer-direct billing are accepted, or a separately authorized commercial pathway is documented.
- [ ] Vendor plan supports business use, named users, MFA, administrative roles and required controls.
- [ ] Dirtyworks.ai is permitted to administer/support the account under vendor terms and customer authorization.
- [ ] Required logs, exports, user/seat data, usage data, invoices and renewal facts are available at an acceptable manual effort.
- [ ] Existing identity, MSP and security administration seams are understood.
- [ ] A safe suspension, revocation, export and offboarding path exists.
- [ ] Any data integration, hosted AI, API, custom agent, application or production workflow is identified as separate advanced scope.

## 4. Data, security, privacy and risk

- [ ] Data categories are inventoried: public, internal, confidential, personal, privileged, regulated, safety-related and high-impact.
- [ ] First scope excludes unsupported sensitive, safety-critical, employment-decision, professional-decision or other high-impact use.
- [ ] Intended collection, access, model use, logging, retention, deletion and processing location are documented.
- [ ] Customer understands vendor training/data-use settings and material subprocessors.
- [ ] Named least-privilege customer access can be issued without shared credentials.
- [ ] Employee/user notice, monitoring and analytics implications are customer-approved where applicable.
- [ ] Privacy, security, legal, insurance or sector-specialist review triggers have been evaluated.
- [ ] No unresolved permission leakage, prohibited data/use or inability to contain the service exists.

## 5. Delivery and support fit

- [ ] In-scope users, administrators, products, locations and expected joiner/mover/leaver volume are estimated.
- [ ] Onboarding, training, support, administration, reporting and renewal responsibilities are assigned.
- [ ] Support hours, priorities, response targets, exclusions and vendor/customer dependency treatment are accepted.
- [ ] Customer contacts and alternates exist for normal support and P1 incidents.
- [ ] Training audience, format, attendance ownership and refresher expectations are feasible.
- [ ] Customer can supply roster, tenant access, invoice/contract records and required approvals on schedule.
- [ ] Founder/contractor capacity supports launch, 30-day stabilization and recurring service without harming existing commitments.

## 6. Cost, billing and commercial fit

- [ ] Vendor licence/usage costs and Dirtyworks.ai service fees are shown separately.
- [ ] The first version of the product register captures plan, quantity, users/admins, price, currency, tax treatment, billing period, renewal/cancellation and evidence source.
- [ ] Manual monthly reconciliation method is agreed: vendor invoice/portal export plus customer-authorized finance evidence where available.
- [ ] The customer understands that a consolidated cost statement is not necessarily a single invoice.
- [ ] Setup, recurring, out-of-scope/change, travel and applicable tax terms are defined.
- [ ] Delivery hours, direct platform cost, contractor cost and contingency preserve the required margin or have an approved experiment exception.
- [ ] Payment method, deposit/advance, invoice timing, PO needs, late payment and suspension rules are workable.
- [ ] Dirtyworks.ai will not carry uncapped variable vendor consumption, credit or pass-through exposure without explicit approval and controls.

## 7. Contract and launch readiness

- [ ] Counsel-approved MSA, Order Form and Data/Security/AI Schedule match this scope.
- [ ] Managed assets, exclusions, acceptance, customer dependencies, change allowance and offboarding are complete.
- [ ] Products, third parties, subprocessors, processing instructions and data-location position are recorded.
- [ ] Liability, indemnity, insurance, IP, confidentiality, renewal, termination and incident provisions fit the actual risk.
- [ ] Customer and Dirtyworks.ai signatories and effective/start dates are clear.
- [ ] No work starts before required signature, initial payment and access authorization.

## 8. Acceptance decision

Select one:

- [ ] **GO** — repeatable core scope; requirements, risk, margin and capacity are acceptable.
- [ ] **CONDITIONAL GO** — only the recorded conditions may remain; owners and expiry dates are assigned.
- [ ] **PAID DISCOVERY** — value may exist, but effort, data, product or risk must be resolved before a managed commitment.
- [ ] **DECLINE / REFER** — outside service boundary, risk appetite, authority, margin or capacity.

Automatic `DECLINE`, `REFER` or `PAID DISCOVERY` triggers include unsupported resale; unclear authority; shared-admin-only access; prohibited/high-impact first use; unknown material data flow; no safe suspension/offboarding; unbounded custom integration hidden inside core service; an unsupported service promise; or delivery economics that depend on unmeasured automation.
