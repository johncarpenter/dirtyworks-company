Managed knowledge and AI operations. The brand argues that the unglamorous work — sources, permissions, evaluation, ownership — is the product. Every claim in this guide is a rule, not a mood.

01 / VoiceDirtyworks.ai

Say the
unglamorous
thing first.

WE SOUND LIKE
  • Plain, specific, verifiable. Numbers or nothing.
  • Named owners and dates, not "seamless" and "robust".
  • Limitations published before credentials.
  • "I don't know" treated as a feature.
WE NEVER SOUND LIKE
  • Transformation, journey, unlock, revolutionise.
  • Autopilot, magic, effortless, AI-powered anything.
  • Metrics without a measurement method.
  • Case studies we cannot evidence.

Orange is action and open gaps. Blueprint is evidence and sources. Acid is verification only — at 2% it must stay rare enough to mean something. Red (#C1200B) is a semantic error state, never a brand colour.

07 / PhotographyEditorial documentary
SHOW
  • People absorbed in real work — never looking at camera.
  • Experienced workers and mixed-age teams.
  • Hands marking, comparing, teaching, resolving.
  • Paper and digital tools in the same frame.
  • Worn wood, binders, wool, steel, cables.
  • Hard side light, subtle grain, real skin texture.
  • Negative space that can take a solid capsule.
AVOID
  • Smiles at camera, handshakes, boardroom lineups.
  • Glowing brains, robots, holograms, purple gradients.
  • Glass tech campuses, identical young startup teams.
  • Fake PPE, hard hats indoors, oil-derrick Calgary.
  • Readable confidential documents or credentials.
  • Soft gradient scrims over photography.
  • Any image implied as customer proof without a release.

Text over image sits on a solid, square-cornered bone or ink capsule — never a gradient scrim. Commission real photography before public launch.

08 / Non-negotiablesDirtyworks.ai

Ten rules that
outrank taste.

  • Hold the colour ratio: 55 bone / 25 ink / 12 orange / 6 blueprint / 2 acid.
  • Never invent a colour outside the five plus derived neutrals.
  • Square corners. 0–3px radius. Hard rules, no soft shadows.
  • Colour never means anything alone — pair it with a mono label.
  • Every metric carries its status: MEASURED, HYPOTHESIS, or ILLUSTRATIVE.
  • Motif before pictogram; an icon is an affordance, not decoration.
  • Line icons only — never filled, rounded, mixed, or emoji.
  • No gradient scrims over photography; use a solid capsule.
  • Generated imagery is editorial only, never customer proof.
  • Publish the limitation before the credential.
