## Assumptions and production decisions

- The finished ad is nine seconds: an eight-second image-to-video shot plus a one-second static outro. This keeps the requested coach line at seven seconds and preserves the required one-second closing hold.
- The outro is produced in editing software, not by the image or video model. It uses a blurred version of the ending anchor so the exact copy remains stable.
- `AI` is completely offscreen. The loose gloves and empty jersey imply the unseen player's uncontrolled disclosure while preserving the campaign rule that `AI`'s face is never shown.
- Three recurring players are used instead of four. In 9:16 this keeps expressions and foreground equipment readable.
- The offscreen collision is loud but causes no visible injury, cry of pain, blood, or fighting. The joke concerns poor operating judgment, not player safety.
- Camera remains completely static. The movement comes from synchronized head turns, bench reactions, and the equipment sliding through the foreground.
