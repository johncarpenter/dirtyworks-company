# Dirtyworks.ai campaign concepts v2

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Mode:** Built-in image generation  
**Status:** Second-round concept tests; fictional people, products, and events; not customer proof

## Direction decisions

- Drop the robot/trades route.
- Continue the early-1980s hockey route with `AI` personified as a human player.
- Continue the luxury/fashion route as the simpler scalable campaign system.
- Do not generate vendor logos. For production ads, composite approved official marks into the blank medallion and verify each vendor's current trademark rules. A vendor mark identifies the product; it must not imply campaign sponsorship or endorsement.

## 01 — Human AI joins the team

**Narrative mechanism:** `AI` is an exceptionally talented human player who lacks team context. He executes brilliantly, then makes a simple situational mistake. The team needs coaching, operating discipline, and somebody responsible for the whole game—not less talent.

**Key frame:** AI celebrates after scoring on his own team.

**Series arc:**

1. `The signing` — the struggling club unveils AI as its impossible new star.
2. `The own goal` — perfect execution in the wrong direction.
3. `The playbook` — AI memorizes every system but follows the opponent's version.
4. `The line change` — AI optimizes his shift and forgets the rest of the team exists.
5. `The coach` — the team finally gives capability the context and operation it needs.

**Copy territory:** `AI needs a coach.` / `Talent is not an operating model.` / `Somebody still has to run the team.`

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: narrative key frame and website editorial image for a humorous early-1980s underdog hockey campaign
Primary request: Create a photorealistic freeze-frame from a fictional low-budget Canadian hockey comedy made in 1983. A struggling adult minor-league team has recruited a mysterious new human player named “AI”—not a robot. He is clearly the fastest, most technically gifted player on the ice, but has just scored a beautiful goal into his own team’s net. AI skates away celebrating with total confidence while his matching-uniform goalie and teammates react with exhausted disbelief and point toward the other end. The humour is dry and character-based: extraordinary ability, no situational context.
Scene/backdrop: dingy small-town community arena, scuffed boards, sparse tired crowd, old sodium and fluorescent rink lighting, slightly fogged glass; fictional team with plain mismatched uniforms and no real logos.
Subject: AI is an adult human athlete, charismatic and unusually polished compared with the scrappy team, no cybernetic features. His back is partly toward camera so the jersey nameplate is visible and must read exactly “AI” using the two capital letters A and I, never “AL” and never “A1”. The puck is visibly resting inside the goal behind him. Teammates wear the same jersey colors so it is clear he scored on his own side.
Style/medium: period-authentic 1983 sports-comedy film frame transferred to worn home video; 4:3 aspect ratio, 16mm grain, slightly soft zoom lens, muted color, fluorescent green cast, analog chroma bleed, mild gate weave and tracking noise; natural physical comedy rather than modern parody.
Composition/framing: medium-wide rink-level shot; celebrating AI in the foreground with readable two-letter nameplate; own-team net and puck clearly visible behind; goalie and two teammates create a reaction triangle; imperfect broadcast crop with room near the boards for separately typeset copy.
Lighting/mood: affectionate, scrappy, rueful and funny; the team is poorly run but human, not cruel or incompetent.
Text (verbatim): “AI” appears exactly once on the back jersey nameplate, as A followed by capital I.
Constraints: no other readable text, no logos, no trademarks, no celebrity likenesses, no recognizable uniforms, characters, hairstyles or scenes from any existing hockey movie, no watermark; no robot, android, glowing eyes, circuitry or futuristic effects; no injury or dangerous play.
Avoid: direct imitation of Slap Shot or any other film, famous hockey characters, NHL branding, broad slapstick faces, polished modern retro fashion shoot, synthwave neon, cinematic superhero action, blood, fighting, sexual content, modern equipment or smartphones.
```

## 02 — AI should not be a luxury

**Campaign mechanism:** each major AI product is photographed like an unattainable fragrance or fashion object. The tool remains desirable and useful; physical invoices, extravagant staging, and a tiny ordinary output expose the uncontrolled operating cost.

**Repeatable production system:**

- identical black studio, marble plinth, receipt ribbon, orange power cord, and copy position;
- one original neutral “product bottle” with a blank medallion;
- approved official vendor mark composited into the medallion only after trademark review;
- one product-specific ordinary output placed beside it;
- optional fashion/location films for larger campaign beats, while simple studio shots carry the regular series.

**Copy territory:** `AI shouldn't be a luxury.` / `Useful does not have to mean extravagant.` / `Keep the intelligence. Lose the excess.` / `The tool is good. The operating model is expensive.`

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical luxury product-shot template for a short-form “AI should not be a luxury” campaign and website editorial imagery
Primary request: Create a photorealistic, over-produced fragrance advertisement for a fictional AI subscription. A flawless obsidian-black glass object—half premium perfume bottle, half minimal software appliance—stands on a monumental veined white-marble plinth. It is genuinely beautiful and desirable, but an absurd ribbon of fine ivory receipts emerges from beneath it, coils around the plinth, and disappears off-frame. Beside the expensive object sits its entire practical output: one small ordinary warm-paper note. AI is presented as useful; the extravagance and uncontrolled cost are the problem.
Scene/backdrop: black studio void with theatrical mist, wet reflective floor, a single windblown black silk panel and no recognizable location.
Subject: one original unbranded black-glass AI product object with a generous flat blank front medallion intended for an official vendor mark to be composited later; one tiny note; long invoice ribbon with completely unreadable marks; one restrained signal-orange power cord.
Style/medium: photorealistic high-fashion fragrance product photography, premium magazine campaign, 80mm studio lens, immaculate material rendering, silver-gelatin near-monochrome, hard sculpting rim lights, deep blacks, controlled specular highlights, fine film grain; satire played completely straight.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5 social-ad composition; product centered slightly above the midpoint, vast negative black field at top for separately typeset headline, receipt ribbon creates an elegant S-curve through the foreground, tiny note clearly visible as scale contrast.
Lighting/mood: severe, seductive, aloof, absurdly expensive, sophisticated rather than campy.
Color palette: black glass, silver, white marble, ivory paper, with only the small Dirtyworks.ai signal-orange cable in selective color.
Materials/textures: obsidian glass, chrome cap detail, veined marble, satin, fine receipt paper, wet lacquered floor.
Constraints: no embedded words, no generated logo, no trademarks, no recognizable perfume packaging, no watermark, no readable prices or invoice details, no UI; preserve a clean blank label/medallion area for later compositing of an official vendor mark; visual must work before copy is added.
Avoid: counterfeit vendor logos, recognizable luxury-brand styling, gold bars, raining money, dollar signs, comedy facial expressions, visible robot, cyberpunk neon, purple gradients, generic sci-fi, cheap product render, clutter, floral perfume clichés, sexualized imagery.
```
