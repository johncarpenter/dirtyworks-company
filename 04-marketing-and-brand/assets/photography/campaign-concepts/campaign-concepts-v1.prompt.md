# Dirtyworks.ai campaign concepts v1

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Mode:** Built-in image generation  
**Status:** Exploratory campaign imagery; fictional people, products, and events; not customer proof

**Decision update — 2026-08-26:** Concept 02, “AI, newest worker on site,” is dropped from further development. A robot-led device risks confusing the managed-operations message and over-indexes on trades when the audience also includes professional services. The exploratory image remains preserved for provenance only.

## Concept mechanisms

### 01 — AI in the 80s

**Series rule:** period-authentic situations receive absurdly over-engineered AI help. The recurring visual signature is consumer VHS footage, a beige computer, and dead-serious reactions.

**Key frame:** a recreational hockey team treats a computer and dot-matrix printer as its assistant coach.

**Possible follow-ons:** bowling strategy night; curling club analytics; sales forecast at a wood-panelled office; an AI-assisted family road trip; inventory optimization at a video store.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: key campaign frame and website editorial image for a humorous “What if we had AI in the 80s?” series
Primary request: A funny, believable home-video still from 1987: an adult recreational hockey coach in a cramped community-rink dressing room has brought a bulky beige computer, green CRT monitor, and dot-matrix printer to act as the team’s new assistant coach. The serious coach studies the glowing screen while a few mixed-age players in mismatched period hockey gear lean in with wary curiosity; an absurdly long sheet of perforated printer paper spills across the bench and floor. The humour is deadpan—everyone treats the machine as completely normal.
Scene/backdrop: low-budget Canadian community arena dressing room, painted cinderblock, wooden bench, old hooks, thermos, taped sticks, practical clutter; late-1980s details only.
Subject: coach and three adult rec-league players, naturally awkward, not posing, no celebrity likenesses; beige computer setup perched precariously but safely on a folding equipment table.
Style/medium: photorealistic frame captured from consumer VHS-C home video circa 1987; authentic 4:3 composition, soft analog focus, interlacing, tracking noise at the bottom edge, chroma bleed, warm fluorescent green cast, blown camcorder highlights, coarse film/video grain, slight timestamp area but no actual digits or text.
Composition/framing: imperfect handheld medium-wide shot, camera slightly too low and tilted, one player partially cut off, computer and paper form the focal line; enough uncluttered cinderblock area for separately typeset ad copy.
Lighting/mood: ugly practical fluorescent light and weak on-camera camcorder lamp; affectionate, dry, low-stakes absurdity rather than broad slapstick.
Color palette: nicotine beige, faded teal, burgundy, off-white, dingy arena green with one restrained signal-orange equipment detail.
Constraints: no legible words, no logos, no team trademarks, no watermark, no modern devices or clothing, no dangerous sports action; visual must work without embedded headline text.
Avoid: polished retro fashion shoot, Stranger Things imitation, synthwave neon, perfect cinematic lighting, modern nostalgia filter, professional NHL arena, futuristic robot, glowing hologram, exaggerated facial comedy, parodying a real team or person.
```

### 02 — AI, newest worker on site

**Series rule:** the AI worker demonstrates impossible technical breadth and then misses one painfully basic piece of context. The mistake stays harmless; the human expert supplies judgment rather than becoming the joke.

**Key frame:** the AI organizes an entire shop perfectly, then tries to mate an enormous nut with a tiny bolt.

**Possible follow-ons:** measures everything except the doorway; brings every specialized tool except the common screwdriver; produces a perfect materials list for the wrong room; labels every pipe while missing that the tap is off.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: key campaign frame and website editorial image for a humorous “AI as a worker” series
Primary request: Personify AI as the newest worker in a practical trades shop: a highly capable, human-scale industrial service robot in clean blue coveralls has organized hundreds of hand tools and fasteners into an impossibly precise grid, but is now confidently trying to thread an obviously enormous steel nut onto a comically tiny bolt. An experienced human foreperson watches from nearby with a completely deadpan expression. The visual joke is “encyclopedic knowledge, elementary mistake.”
Scene/backdrop: real small fabrication and maintenance workshop, scarred steel bench, pegboard, vise, bins, practical fluorescent fixtures; no active machinery, heights, sparks, or hazardous operation.
Subject: one original non-branded service robot with useful proportions, expressive posture rather than a cartoon face, wearing clean work coveralls and basic eye protection; one experienced trades foreperson in ordinary workwear, arms resting on the bench, not posing for camera.
Style/medium: photorealistic independent editorial advertising photograph, 35mm lens, hard direct flash mixed with workshop fluorescent light, crisp foreground, fast shadow falloff, natural material texture, slightly awkward candid timing; sophisticated deadpan comedy.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape; robot and mismatched parts prominent in the centre-right, human reaction secondary at left; precisely ordered tool grid contrasts with scarred workshop surfaces; leave a simple dark field at one edge for separately typeset copy.
Lighting/mood: matter-of-fact, dry, bright enough to read the mistake immediately; affectionate rather than menacing.
Color palette: steel, carbon black, dusty blue workwear, warm bone labels with one restrained signal-orange tool.
Materials/textures: machined steel, scratched workbench, worn cotton, rubber, brushed aluminum, real skin.
Constraints: no legible words, no company logos, no trademarks, no watermark; harmless bench-top mistake only; robot is an original fictional design and not similar to a known film or product character; visual must work without embedded headline text.
Avoid: unsafe construction practices, active saws or welding, humiliation of the human worker, evil robot, glossy humanoid android glamour, glowing brain, hologram, cyberpunk neon, generic hardhat stock photo, slapstick injury, dystopia, superhero pose, childish cartoon style.
```

### 03 — AI, the luxury service

**Series rule:** reproduce luxury and fragrance advertising with total sincerity, then reveal that a ruinously expensive AI system has produced a tiny ordinary benefit. Cost is shown through absurd production scale and physical invoices, not financial jargon.

**Key frame:** a monumental black-glass AI console on a volcanic beach produces one tiny note and an invoice ribbon stretching to the horizon.

**Possible follow-ons:** private-jet cabin for a two-line summary; marble spa attended by technicians for one calendar reminder; desert supercar delivering a basic spreadsheet; couture launch event for a chatbot nobody uses.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: key campaign frame and website editorial image for a satirical luxury-noir AI services series
Primary request: Create an over-the-top black-and-white luxury fragrance advertisement, except the “product” is an extravagantly expensive AI service. On a remote black volcanic beach at night, a severe high-fashion model in sculptural couture stands beside a monumental black-glass AI console displayed on a white marble plinth like an enormous perfume bottle. From a tiny slot in the console, an absurdly long ribbon of pale invoices and receipts unspools across the wet sand into the distance, while the actual output is one tiny plain paper note resting alone on the plinth. The cost-versus-benefit joke should reveal itself after the initial glamour.
Scene/backdrop: exotic black-sand coast, wet reflective ground, wind, distant cliffs and sea mist, no recognizable landmark.
Subject: one adult fashion model with dramatic but non-sexualized styling, original black-glass console with no logo or interface, marble plinth, receipt ribbon and tiny result note; no celebrity likeness.
Style/medium: photorealistic high-fashion fragrance campaign, monochrome noir, 50mm anamorphic feel, hard silver moonlight, wet specular highlights, deep crushed blacks, theatrical fog, very fine film grain, luxury magazine production values; one restrained signal-orange cable may remain in selective color as a Dirtyworks.ai accent.
Composition/framing: cinematic wide 16:9 landscape; model and monolith placed off-centre with vast negative sky and mist for separately typeset copy; invoice ribbon creates a sweeping foreground line; strong silhouette and extreme scale contrast.
Lighting/mood: impossibly expensive, aloof, seductive, absurdly serious; the satire is deadpan, not campy.
Color palette: almost entirely silver gelatin black-and-white, polished black glass, white marble and pale paper, with at most one small signal-orange accent.
Materials/textures: wet sand, windblown couture fabric, polished obsidian glass, veined marble, thin receipt paper, sea mist.
Constraints: no embedded words, no logos, no trademarks, no recognizable perfume bottle design, no watermark, no readable invoice details, no UI; visual must work without headline text and must not resemble a specific luxury campaign.
Avoid: glamour nudity, sexualized pose, celebrity likeness, recognizable brand styling, gold-money clichés, raining cash, comedy mugging, cyberpunk neon, purple gradient, generic sci-fi city, evil AI, visible robots, glowing brain, fantasy illustration, cheap parody-poster look.
```
