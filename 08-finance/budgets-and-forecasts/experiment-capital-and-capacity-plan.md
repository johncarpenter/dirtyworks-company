# Experiment capital and founder-capacity plan

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Sponsor constraint:** half-time founder; $5,000–$50,000 available depending on evidence and implementation need

## Operating interpretation

Plan around **20 founder hours per week**, not a discounted full-time roadmap. Capital is released in evidence-gated tranches. Customer assessments, launches, licences, and managed service should fund customer-specific delivery; experiment capital funds reusable company capability and prudent production readiness.

The lower end of the range supports market validation. It does not support a mature production service, custom application, substantial paid acquisition, or broad legal/security certification. The upper end can support two carefully bounded pilots and reusable foundations if founder labour remains the primary delivery capacity.

## Founder capacity by phase

### Phase 1 — Market validation: 20 hours/week

| Work | Hours/week | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Interviews, partner/customer conversations, follow-up | 8 | Primary evidence and pipeline |
| Account/partner research and preparation | 4 | High-quality targeting |
| Proposals, offer assets, and synthesis | 4 | Convert and improve the offer |
| Company operations, finance, documentation | 2 | Keep the business auditable |
| Unplanned work and sponsor decisions | 2 | Prevent a fully loaded schedule |

At this allocation, plan for two to three substantive external conversations per week. Twenty buyer interviews plus MSP discovery will take roughly 8–10 weeks when scheduling and follow-up are included.

### Phase 2 — Two-pilot period: 20 hours/week

| Work | Hours/week | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Customer delivery and quality | 9 | Reviews, configuration, evaluation, launch |
| Sales and partner pipeline | 5 | Avoid a post-pilot pipeline gap |
| Managed operations/support | 2 | Stabilization and incidents |
| Productization/documentation | 2 | Capture reusable process |
| Finance/admin/buffer | 2 | Control and contingency |

Do not run more than two simultaneous launches at half-time founder capacity. If the reference launch consistently needs more than 45 founder hours plus specialist time, schedule sequentially or add funded delivery capacity.

## Evidence-gated capital

### Tranche 1 — Up to $5,000: validate and prepare

**Release:** sponsor-approved now.  
**Objective:** obtain segment, price, and partner evidence without building production infrastructure.

Suggested envelopes—not committed vendor prices:

| Use | Ceiling |
|---|---:|
| Basic corporate/accounting/legal orientation and contract issue list | $1,500 |
| Brand/site prototype and essential business tools | $1,000 |
| Professional liability/cyber insurance advice and quotations | $500 |
| Research, local travel, association/roundtable costs | $1,000 |
| Contingency | $1,000 |

Do not spend this tranche on a custom knowledge platform, paid lead volume, elaborate identity work, or full website production.

### Tranche 2 — Additional $10,000–$15,000: production readiness

**Release gate:** three paid reviews or one paid MSP-sourced review plus one direct paid review; at least two are feasible launches.

Likely uses:

- counsel-reviewed master services, statement-of-work, privacy/data-processing, subcontractor/channel, and acceptable-use terms;
- appropriate commercial, professional, cyber, and privacy insurance;
- secure business accounts, password/access management, service records, and backup/continuity foundations;
- controlled demo/evaluation environments and required software;
- specialist privacy/security review for the exact pilot scopes;
- limited design/development support for the public site and reusable launch assets.

### Tranche 3 — Additional $20,000–$30,000: repeatable delivery

**Release gate:** two accepted launches, at least two recurring contracts, measured value, and a credible path to 50%+ day-90 recurring gross margin.

Likely uses:

- contractor delivery capacity where measured bottlenecks justify it;
- evaluation, reporting, customer-record, and offboarding automation;
- public website and proof assets based on approved claims;
- targeted security hardening or assessment required by the chosen customers/partners;
- MSP enablement assets tied to a volume or revenue commitment;
- working-capital reserve for receivables and vendor timing.

Do not release tranche 3 solely because the first $20,000 has been spent. Evidence, not sunk cost, controls the gate.

## Funding mechanism

The target cash pattern is:

1. Paid review covers review delivery and contributes to acquisition learning.
2. Launch milestone payments fund customer-specific implementation before most work is performed.
3. Recurring service is billed in advance.
4. Partner enablement is paid or backed by signed customer/revenue commitments.
5. Experiment capital funds reusable capability and risk reduction, not chronic underpricing.

## Spend approval thresholds

| Commitment | Approval rule |
|---|---|
| Under $500, within an approved tranche and category | Founder may execute and record |
| $500–$2,500 or any new recurring tool | Record alternatives, annualized cost, cancellation, and evidence removed |
| Above $2,500, annual commitment, contractor, or production-risk dependency | Sponsor decision recorded before commitment |
| Any spend using customer data or production access | Security/privacy and contract review regardless of amount |

## Financial model still required

Before a production launch, build an 18-month base/downside/upside model with:

- founder compensation policy and fully loaded delivery cost;
- corporate, accounting, legal, insurance, software, marketing, contractor, and tax assumptions;
- review, launch, recurring, expansion, direct, and channel revenue cohorts;
- payment timing, sales tax, receivables, refunds, and pass-through costs;
- capacity by offer and partner model;
- gross margin, operating cash flow, runway, and hiring gates;
- downside case with no launch conversion and white-label margin compression.

The sponsor’s range is sufficient to define a staged experiment, but not enough to responsibly invent precise runway until these cost and compensation inputs are known.
