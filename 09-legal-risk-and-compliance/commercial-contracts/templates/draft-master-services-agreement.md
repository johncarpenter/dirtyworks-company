## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **Applicable Law** means laws and binding regulatory requirements applicable to a Party's performance of the Agreement, having regard to that Party's role, location, activities, and information under its control.
- **Authorized User** means a person Customer authorizes to use a Managed Service within the scope of an Order.
- **Customer Data** means information, content, records, personal information, credentials, prompts, instructions, and materials Customer or its users provide or make accessible in connection with the Services, excluding Dirtyworks Background Materials.
- **Deliverable** means a customer-specific item expressly identified as a deliverable in an Order.
- **Dirtyworks Background Materials** means Dirtyworks' pre-existing or independently developed methods, tools, templates, software, prompts, evaluation structures, know-how, processes, documentation, and generalized improvements, including modifications that do not disclose Customer Confidential Information.
- **Documentation** means user, administrator, operating, security, or service documentation Dirtyworks supplies for the applicable Services.
- **Fees** means fees and authorized charges in an Order.
- **Incident** means an event that results in confirmed unauthorized access to, acquisition, use, loss, alteration, or disclosure of Customer Data in Dirtyworks' custody or control, or a material compromise of the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of a Managed Service. The Data, Security and AI Schedule may refine this definition.
- **Managed Service** means a recurring service identified in an Order.
- **Order** means an Order Form, Statement of Work, change order, or other ordering document signed by both Parties under this Agreement.
- **Output** means content generated or materially assisted by an artificial-intelligence system through the Services.
- **Personal Information** has the meaning given by Applicable Law.
- **Services** means the review, implementation, administration, support, training, integration, evaluation, monitoring, knowledge, workflow, product-portfolio, and other services identified in an Order.
- **Supported Use** means an intended user, purpose, domain, source, product, workflow, question/action class, and risk boundary expressly identified as supported in an Order.
- **Third-Party Service** means software, models, cloud, platforms, connectors, data, or services supplied by a party other than Dirtyworks.

## 2. Agreement structure

2.1 This Agreement establishes the terms under which the Parties may enter Orders. Neither Party is required to enter an Order.

2.2 An Order is binding only when signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. Dirtyworks is not required to begin work before the applicable Order is signed, required Fees are paid, and stated customer dependencies are available.

2.3 If documents conflict, the following order applies unless a signed amendment expressly states otherwise: (a) a signed amendment; (b) the applicable Order for its scope, Fees, schedule, acceptance, and expressly identified deviations; (c) the Data, Security and AI Schedule for its subject; (d) an applicable service/product schedule for its subject; (e) this Agreement.

2.4 A Customer purchase order is for administrative convenience only. Its additional or inconsistent terms do not modify the Agreement, even if Dirtyworks references, accepts, or processes the purchase order.

2.5 Proposals, websites, presentations, demonstrations, messages, and other materials are not contractual commitments unless an Order expressly incorporates the specific provision.

## 3. Services and professional standard

3.1 Dirtyworks will perform the Services materially in accordance with the applicable Order and with the reasonable care and skill expected of a provider performing similar technology and managed-service work in comparable circumstances.

3.2 Dirtyworks may use qualified personnel and approved subcontractors. Dirtyworks remains responsible for their performance to the same extent as if Dirtyworks performed the applicable obligation, subject to the Agreement.

3.3 Dates depend on stated assumptions and Customer Dependencies. Dirtyworks will notify Customer when a dependency or material risk is reasonably expected to affect scope, date, Fees, or acceptance. The Parties will use change control rather than silently expanding the Services.

3.4 Dirtyworks may recommend that Customer remediate information, use a simpler non-AI solution, change scope, or not proceed. A review is not defective merely because it recommends no implementation.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

Customer will:

4.1 appoint the executive sponsor, operational contact, knowledge/source owner, and access/security owner required by the Order;

4.2 provide timely, accurate, and complete information, access, decisions, approvals, representative questions, and personnel reasonably needed for the Services;

4.3 own and approve the accuracy, authority, currency, legality, classification, and permitted use of Customer Data and source information;

4.4 obtain and maintain rights, notices, consents, authorizations, and lawful authority required to provide Customer Data, authorize processing, connect systems, monitor use, and permit Authorized Users to participate;

4.5 approve user identities, permission groups, access exceptions, source scope, Supported Uses, and production release;

4.6 maintain Customer-controlled systems, identity, endpoints, networks, accounts, backups, policies, and security responsibilities not expressly assigned to Dirtyworks;

4.7 ensure Authorized Users follow Documentation, use Outputs only within Supported Uses, verify material source information, maintain human accountability, and promptly report errors, misuse, and Incidents;

4.8 not direct Dirtyworks or a Third-Party Service to perform unlawful, deceptive, harmful, unauthorized, excluded, or prohibited processing; and

4.9 pay Fees and Third-Party Service charges as agreed.

Customer is responsible for consequences caused by its failure to meet these responsibilities, including reasonable schedule and change effects, except to the extent caused by Dirtyworks' breach.

## 5. Change control

5.1 A material change to users, sources, permission groups, data types, products, connectors, workflows, decisions, locations, service levels, support windows, risk, schedule, or Deliverables requires a written change order.

5.2 Either Party may request a change. Dirtyworks will describe material effects on scope, Fees, schedule, risk, acceptance, recurring operation, and Third-Party Services before implementation where reasonably practicable.

5.3 Dirtyworks may take temporary emergency action without a change order when reasonably necessary to contain an Incident, prevent unauthorized or harmful operation, or comply with law. Dirtyworks will notify Customer as soon as reasonably practicable and document further work through change control.

## 6. Fees, taxes, and payment

6.1 Customer will pay Fees in Canadian dollars according to the Order. Fees exclude applicable GST/HST and other transaction taxes, which Customer will pay except taxes on Dirtyworks' net income.

6.2 Customer will reimburse pre-approved travel and out-of-pocket expenses. Legal, privacy, security, specialist, custom connector, data-remediation, and Third-Party Service charges are separate unless included in the Order.

6.3 Undisputed invoices are due `[15/30]` days after invoice date, except reviews paid in advance and milestone Fees stated otherwise. Customer must dispute an invoice in writing with reasonable detail within `[10]` days and pay the undisputed portion when due.

6.4 Overdue amounts accrue interest at the lesser of `[1.5% per month]` and the maximum lawful rate. After written notice and a reasonable cure period, Dirtyworks may suspend affected Services for non-payment. Customer remains responsible for committed Fees and reasonable restart/remediation costs.

6.5 Customer-caused delay does not defer Fees for completed milestones or reserved capacity identified in an Order. If a delay exceeds `[15/30]` days, Dirtyworks may reschedule work and issue a change order.

6.6 Recurring Fees are invoiced monthly in advance. Usage, pass-through, and approved variable charges may be invoiced in arrears.

## 7. Third-Party Services

7.1 Unless an Order expressly states otherwise, Customer contracts and pays the Third-Party Service provider directly, owns the tenant/account, and accepts the provider's terms. Dirtyworks is authorized to administer the service only within the Order.

7.2 An Order will identify the commercial route: customer-direct, authorized resale, customer marketplace/cloud, partner, licence-reviewed deployment, or other approved path. Dirtyworks is not a reseller or seller of record merely because it recommends, configures, supports, or includes a Third-Party Service in an estimate.

7.3 Customer acknowledges that Third-Party Services may change features, models, prices, limits, terms, data locations, subprocessors, availability, and security. Dirtyworks will provide the review/notice obligations expressly stated in the Order but does not control or warrant a Third-Party Service.

7.4 Third-party downtime, errors, limits, acts, omissions, and contract changes are not a breach by Dirtyworks, except to the extent Dirtyworks fails to perform its express configuration, administration, monitoring, notice, or mitigation obligations.

7.5 Dirtyworks may recommend replacement, suspension, scope reduction, or termination when a Third-Party Service no longer meets an approved requirement. Resulting work beyond included capacity requires change control.

## 8. Access and credentials

8.1 Customer will provide delegated, named, least-privilege access where commercially available. Shared credentials are prohibited unless expressly approved with compensating controls.

8.2 Each Party will protect credentials, use multi-factor authentication where supported and appropriate, promptly revoke unneeded access, and notify the other of suspected compromise affecting the Services.

8.3 Dirtyworks will use Customer access only to perform the Services, provide support, prevent or investigate an Incident, or comply with Customer's documented instruction and Applicable Law.

8.4 Customer may not provide Dirtyworks with access broader than the approved scope. Dirtyworks may refuse or return unnecessarily sensitive information or access.

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 **Confidential Information** means non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable recipient should understand is confidential, including Customer Data, security information, business plans, pricing, methods, credentials, and the Agreement.

9.2 The receiving Party will: (a) use Confidential Information only to perform or receive the Services and exercise rights under the Agreement; (b) protect it using at least reasonable care and no less care than it uses for similar information; and (c) disclose it only to personnel, professional advisers, insurers, financiers, and approved subcontractors who need it and are bound by protective duties.

9.3 Confidential Information excludes information the recipient can demonstrate: (a) is lawfully public without breach; (b) was lawfully known without restriction before disclosure; (c) is received lawfully from a third party without duty; or (d) is independently developed without use of the discloser's Confidential Information.

9.4 If disclosure is legally required, the recipient will, where lawful, give prompt notice and reasonable assistance so the discloser may seek protection. The recipient will disclose only what is legally required.

9.5 On request or termination, the recipient will return or destroy Confidential Information as required by the Agreement, subject to legal retention, secure backups, professional records, and the Data, Security and AI Schedule.

9.6 These duties continue for `[five]` years after disclosure, except trade secrets, credentials, and Personal Information remain protected for as long as their nature and Applicable Law require. Counsel must confirm the duration.

## 10. Privacy, security, and Incidents

10.1 Each Party will comply with Applicable Law for the Personal Information under its control and its role in the Services.

10.2 Where Dirtyworks processes Personal Information for Customer, the [Data, Security and AI Schedule](draft-data-security-and-ai-schedule.md), completed for the applicable Order, applies.

10.3 Dirtyworks will maintain the security controls expressly stated in that Schedule. No statement in the Agreement represents that Dirtyworks is certified to ISO 27001, SOC 2, CyberSecure Canada, or another standard unless the applicable current certification is expressly identified.

10.4 Dirtyworks will notify Customer of an Incident without undue delay and within the contract-specific period stated in the Schedule after Dirtyworks confirms that an Incident affecting Customer Data has occurred. Initial notice may contain incomplete information and will be updated.

10.5 Each Party will contain, investigate, preserve evidence, remediate, and cooperate according to its control and legal role. Unless Applicable Law assigns otherwise, Customer remains responsible for determining notification to individuals, regulators, customers, employees, or others, with Dirtyworks providing reasonably available facts and assistance. Additional assistance caused by Customer systems, instructions, or legal requirements may be chargeable unless the Incident resulted from Dirtyworks' breach.

11.4 Third-Party Service use, retention, and training of Customer Data are governed by the approved product configuration and disclosed third-party terms. 11.5 Dirtyworks may create and use aggregated or de-identified operational information only if the applicable Schedule permits it and reasonable measures prevent re-identification and disclosure of Customer Confidential Information. Such information will not be used as a public case study or identify Customer without separate written permission.
